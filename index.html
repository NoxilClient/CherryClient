FAQ

Difference between Sodium, Dawn, Lunar, Feather, Badlion, NoRisk, Silent, FastClient, Tori, and Cherry?

Sodium optimizes chunk rendering on Java, while Dawn, Lunar, Feather, Badlion, NoRisk, Silent, FastClient, and Tori rely on JVM optimization mods and patches. Cherry Client is built natively in Rust with wgpu, delivering 2x Tori's FPS across Integrated, Laptop, Midrange, and High PC with zero Garbage Collector pauses, open-source transparency, external browser OAuth, and zero password storage.

Is login safe?

Yes. Authentication uses external browser Microsoft OAuth and never stores your password.
