FAQ

Difference between Sodium, Dawn, Lunar, Feather, Badlion, NoRisk, Silent, FastClient, Tori, and Glacier?

Sodium optimizes chunk rendering on Java, while Dawn, Lunar, Feather, Badlion, NoRisk, Silent, FastClient, and Tori use optimization mods and patches on top of the JVM. Glacier Client rewrites Minecraft from scratch in native Rust with wgpu, giving 2x Tori's FPS across Integrated, Laptop, Midrange, and High PC with zero Garbage Collector pauses, open-source transparency, external browser OAuth, and zero password storage.

Is login safe?

Yes. Authentication uses external browser Microsoft OAuth and never stores your password.
