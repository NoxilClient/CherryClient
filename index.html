Privacy Policy & Terms

🔒 Zero Credential Collection

We never collect, log, or store your Microsoft or Minecraft passwords. Authentication happens exclusively through official Microsoft OAuth tokens stored locally on your machine.

🛡️ No Invasive Telemetry

Cherry Client does not sell user data, track browsing activity, or run background profiling. Minimal crash diagnostics are strictly opt-in and anonymized.

💾 Local-First Configuration

Your module toggles, HUD layout, and visual profiles stay locally inside your Cherry directory unless you explicitly export or share them.

🧹 Instant Data Removal

Uninstalling Cherry Client removes all local session caches and config files immediately—no hidden background services or residual trackers.
